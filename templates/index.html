How It Works

Simple steps to help reunite families

1

Report a Case

Submit details of a missing or found person with photos and last known location.

2

We Verify

Our team and partner NGOs verify the information for accuracy and safety.

3

Public Visibility

Cases appear on the portal so the whole community can help search.

4

Reunion

When a match is found, we connect families with authorities and support the reunion.
